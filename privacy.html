Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

LeadWake Automations respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when individuals interact with LeadWake Automations or with communication systems operated through LeadWake Automations.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with a participating business or LeadWake Automations system, information may include:

  • Your phone number
  • Call-related information
  • SMS messages you send or receive
  • Information you voluntarily provide during a conversation
  • Technical information necessary to operate and maintain the service

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide requested communication services, respond to inquiries, support customer follow-up, operate automated workflows, improve service reliability, and maintain appropriate records.

SMS Messaging and Consent

SMS messages are sent only after the recipient has provided consent to receive text communication. For missed-call recovery workflows, consent may be provided through an automated voice prompt in which the caller is asked whether they would like to continue by text and affirmatively selects the designated option.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

Information Sharing

LeadWake Automations does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate communication, automation, hosting, messaging, scheduling, or related systems.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, clients, or the service.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and compliance records, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. You may request assistance by replying HELP.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact LeadWake Automations using the contact information published on our website.
